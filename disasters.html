Types of Disasters

Learn about different natural disasters and the safety measures that can help reduce their impact.

Flood

An overflow of water that submerges normally dry land, often caused by heavy rainfall, rivers, or coastal storms.

Earthquake

A sudden shaking of the Earth's surface caused by the movement of rocks along faults beneath the ground.

Cyclone

A large rotating storm system that can bring strong winds, heavy rainfall, storm surges, and flooding.

Forest Fire

An uncontrolled fire that spreads through forests, grasslands, or other areas containing vegetation.

Tsunami

A series of powerful ocean waves usually caused by an underwater earthquake, volcanic activity, or landslide.

Landslide

The movement of rock, soil, or debris down a slope, commonly triggered by heavy rainfall, earthquakes, or erosion.
